# Opaque — brand

## Product and audience

Opaque is a security control layer for AI agents. Platform and security teams
use it to gate permitted actions, require approval, bound authority and inspect
recorded outcomes. Credential custody supports those controls.

The entry-page statement is:

> **Gate and audit agents at scale.**

Use it in the homepage hero and search content. The site description states the
same purpose with concrete behaviors. Keep the headline readable; do not cover
product meaning with a redaction animation or add a second slogan.

## Public copy

The homepage answers: what does Opaque control, and how do I try it?
Lead with the controls and show one real command workflow. Aim for 300 words of
prose. Each section adds a behavior, prerequisite, evidence or next action.
Link configuration and protocol detail to reference pages. Avoid persona cards
and repeated walkthroughs.

State availability where capabilities are introduced. Signed policy distribution
and SIEM export are public-core capabilities; fleet collectors and management
views belong to the separate enterprise product. Do not imply that a hosted
fleet control plane comes with the public core.

Claims apply to operations routed through the broker. State the separate-identity
custody requirement, direct-access limits and uncertain-outcome semantics.
Distinguish recorded evidence from provider completion. Label synthetic demos
and illustrations. Do not invent metrics, customers or certifications.

## Documentation

Each page answers one operator question. Architecture pages show a pattern and
its trust boundary. Workflow pages show prerequisites, commands and how to inspect
the outcome. Reference pages preserve exact fields and failure semantics without
repeating the overview. Remove introductions and conclusions that restate another
section. Keep proposed capabilities and historical records visibly separate from
released behavior. Reference length follows the contract, not a word quota.

## Voice

Use short declarative sentences and concrete nouns. Name what the operator can
do. Explain security claims through the applicable control and deployment
boundary. Keep commands, config keys, references and IDs in monospace.

## Visual foundation

Shared tokens and licensed local fonts live in `assets/brand/opaque.css`;
`docs/stylesheets/extra.css` applies them to the site.

- **Type:** Archivo for prose, IBM Plex Mono for commands and machine output.
  Headline tracking is negative; body tracking is not.
- **Ground:** graphite in dark mode, warm paper in light mode. Verify both.
- **Accent:** one amber seal accent for links and approval markers.
- **Mark:** the redaction glyph remains the brand mark. Keep all product copy
  readable and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- **Semantic color:** green means a verified state, never decoration.

Preserve the shared design when editing copy. Review the rendered hierarchy,
working links and mobile navigation before publication.
